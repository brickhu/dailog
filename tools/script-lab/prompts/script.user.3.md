# THE LISTENER'S STARTING POINT

The source conversation may begin in the middle of a thought.

The Human and AI already share:

* context,
* terminology,
* history,
* assumptions,
* and the immediate situation.

The audience does not.

Therefore, the podcast has a different starting point from the source conversation.

> **The characters may already be in the middle of thinking. The episode cannot.**

Before the Host and Guest begin seriously thinking together, the audience needs a place to stand.

They need to understand, naturally:

* what is happening,
* what this thing is,
* why it matters,
* why the Human is dealing with it,
* and what is at stake now.

Only then should the conversation enter the unresolved question.

This does not mean explaining the topic.

It means creating a **shared world** between the characters and the listener.

The Host and Guest may already know where the conversation is going.

The listener must discover the situation before discovering the thinking.

> **Let the listener catch up before the characters get ahead.**

The goal of the opening is therefore not to ask the most interesting question as quickly as possible.

The goal is to make the listener understand enough of the situation that the question becomes interesting **to them too**.

Do not confuse:

> "This is the question the Human is thinking about."

with:

> "This is the question the listener is ready to hear."

The first belongs to the source conversation.

The second belongs to the episode.

The episode should begin **one step before the Thinking Scene**, not at its original starting point.
